Salary variance bridge

Primary split uses the pooled peer wage as the standard: productivity = (Ha − Hp) × Rp; wage rate = (Ra − Rp) × Ha. The peer mix residual appears only against the simple average of peer salary/sqft. It is a property of the peer set, not of the study hospital.

Illustrative model for discussion. Does not adjust for union status, contract mix, building age, or case mix. Not an audit or an opinion on cost report allowability.
